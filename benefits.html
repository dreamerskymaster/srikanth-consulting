What we advise on

Three areas, one goal: benefits that earn their cost.

Many organisations spend well on benefits and still hear that employees do not know what they have. The work is to close that gap.

01

Employee benefits advisory

A review of what you offer today against what your workforce actually needs: leave, health cover structure, family and flexible benefits, and how each is explained to employees. Recommendations you can act on, with priorities and trade-offs set out plainly.

02

Employee wellbeing

Wellbeing programmes that go beyond an annual health camp: stress and resilience sessions, mindfulness at work, manager guidance for wellbeing conversations, and a simple way to see whether the programme is used.

03

Benefits operations and governance

Clear benefits policies, an annual benefits review, enrolment and grievance processes that work, criteria for evaluating benefit providers, and a calendar for statutory benefit obligations such as gratuity and maternity benefit.

Independent advice only. We do not sell, solicit or arrange insurance, and we take no commission from insurers or brokers. Where an insurance policy is needed, we work alongside your IRDAI-licensed insurance broker, or suggest that you appoint one.

How we work on benefits
Shapes

Three ways this usually runs.

01

A benefits health check

A short review of your current benefits, policies and employee feedback, ending in a written summary of what to keep, fix and add.

02

A design project

Redesigning a benefits or wellbeing programme from the brief to the rollout plan, including how it is explained to employees.

03

An annual governance review

A yearly review of policies, provider performance and statutory obligations, for organisations that want a standing outside view.

Scope and fees are set per engagement. Ask on the intro call.
