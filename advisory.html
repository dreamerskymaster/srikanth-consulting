Advisory & Coaching

The work that does not fit in a workshop.

Some problems need a room full of people and a curriculum. Others need one person, one hour at a time, over months. This is the second kind: one to one coaching, and advisory that stays with an organisation long enough to see a decision through.

Book a 15 minute intro call See the two tracks
Track one

Mindful leadership coaching, one to one.

For professionals and senior leaders working on clarity rather than technique. Typical reasons people start: a step up into a role that needs more presence than expertise, a decision that has been pending too long, or the particular flatness that arrives after a long run of delivery.

  • Career clarity and the next move, thought through rather than reacted to
  • Executive presence: how you enter a room, hold a position, and handle being disagreed with
  • Composure under sustained load, using mindfulness as a working tool rather than a retreat
  • Direct work with Srikanth throughout, with no associate handover
Happiness Coaching, Berkeley Institute of Well Being Mindfulness Coaching, Happiitude Business Storytelling, NIST
Track two

Retained HR advisory.

For organisations that need senior HR judgement regularly, but not a full time senior HR hire.

Operations

HR operations and policy

Process design, policy drafting and review, compliance routines, and the unglamorous work of making HR run predictably.

Talent

Hiring and performance

Talent acquisition, performance management frameworks, compensation benchmarking, and onboarding that does not lose people in the first ninety days.

Systems

HR digitalisation

Selection and rollout of HRIS and HCM platforms, including SAP SuccessFactors, with attention to adoption rather than configuration alone.

Culture

Engagement and retention

Diagnosing why people actually leave, and the small number of changes that move it. Culture work with a measurable target, not a values poster.

Compliance

POSH and ESG

POSH framework setup and committee training, and ESG reporting groundwork from a people and governance angle.

Onboarding

Group onboarding design

Designing intake programmes for cohorts rather than individuals, drawing on a flagship group onboarding programme built and run inside a large engineering organisation.

Shapes

Three ways this usually runs.

01

A single session

One conversation on a specific decision. Useful when you want a second opinion from outside your own organisation, and nothing more.

02

A block of sessions

A set number of coaching conversations over a few months, with an agreed focus at the start and a review at the end.

03

A monthly retainer

A fixed amount of senior HR time each month for organisations that would rather have judgement on call than a project.

Scope and fees are set per engagement. Ask on the intro call.

Start with a conversation, not a proposal.

Fifteen minutes is usually enough to tell whether this is the right kind of help.
