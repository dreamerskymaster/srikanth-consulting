He made the campus-to-corporate jump himself. Now he prepares students for theirs.

The short version

A mechanical engineer who chose people over machines, he started on the shop floor at Kirloskar Pneumatics and never lost the engineer's instinct for process. Moved into Human Resources in 1998 with Owens Corning India, and earned a Post Graduate Diploma in HR Management from Welingkar Institute while working full time. Since 2008 in HR leadership with a global engineering group, most recently as Deputy General Manager, Human Resources. Over two decades on the hiring side of the table: talent acquisition, performance management, onboarding, compliance, and HR digitalisation. The transition he teaches is the one he lived.
